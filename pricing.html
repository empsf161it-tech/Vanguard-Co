Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries regarding retainer engagement models, fee caps, and billing protocols.

How are retainer funds held?

All upfront retainer funds are held in segregated, audited IOLTA trust accounts and drawn down only as work is performed and invoiced.

Do you accept contingency or hybrid fee arrangements?

Yes, for qualified commercial litigation claims exceeding $50 Million in probable damages, we offer hybrid retainer-contingency structures.

What payment methods are supported?

We support domestic and international wire transfers, ACH, credit cards, and escrow deposits via our secure client portal.

Are court filing fees and expert witness costs included in retainers?

Disbursements for court filings, expert witness retainers, and travel are itemized separately and billed against your trust ledger with pre-approved budget caps.

Can retainers be paused or transferred between legal matters?

Unused retainer balances can be paused or reallocated to new corporate matters at any time upon written notice from your authorized corporate officer.
