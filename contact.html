Pre-Engagement Intake FAQ

What to expect during your initial consultation with senior counsel.

Is the initial consultation covered under Attorney-Client Privilege?

Yes, under applicable professional conduct rules, all preliminary communications remain strictly confidential regardless of whether an engagement agreement is subsequently executed.

How quickly can conflict checks be cleared?

Our automated compliance software performs worldwide conflict checks across all office jurisdictions within 45 minutes of intake submission.

What documentation should I prepare before our briefing?

Please bring relevant contracts, dispute correspondence, regulatory notices, and corporate formation documents. All files can be uploaded securely via our client portal.

Do you represent corporate entities in urgent injunction proceedings?

Yes, our emergency litigation team is available 24/7 for immediate asset freezing orders, ex-parte injunctions, temporary restraining orders (TROs), and search warrants across global jurisdictions.
